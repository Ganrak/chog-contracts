    Privacy
    • No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no trackers. The code and the fonts all come from this site.
    • Your game progress is saved in your own browser. "Reset demo" in the account menu erases it.
    • When you connect, our server keeps only what the game needs: your public wallet address, the hunter name you pick, your weekly boss scores and your Chog's story. A story is stored with the token and anyone can read it, like the NFT itself.
    • To see which Chogs you hold, your browser reads the public Monad blockchain and loads Chog art from IPFS.
    • We never ask for your email or seed phrase, and never sell or share your data.
    • The site is hosted on Netlify, which keeps standard request logs (such as IP addresses) to run it.
